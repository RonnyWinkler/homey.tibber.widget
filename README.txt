This app expands your Homey dashboard with a handy widget for the Tibber app.

The widget displays a chart of current electricity prices as well as live consumption from your Tibber Pulse electricity meter.
This way, you can keep an eye on your energy consumption and electricity costs at any time – directly on your Homey dashboard!

Requirements:
- Tibber app version 1.10.0 or higher - currently test version
- Tibber Pulse device for energy data (smart meter)
- Tibber Home device for price data and charts
